# Student guide / 学生使用指南

## Choose the right book / 选对课本

Open Vocabulary, select your book, then your unit. Check the book name and unit in the lesson heading before starting. They remain visible during Study, Practice, Cards and Test. HSE package titles keep their existing format. The app remembers your book/unit selection and last page on this device.

进入词汇，选择课本和单元。开始前核对标题中的书名与单元；学习、练习、卡片和测试过程中都会显示。高中词汇包保持原标题格式。应用在此设备上记住书本、单元及上次页面。

## Vocabulary modes / 词汇模式

- **Study / 学习:** Browse meanings and examples; use pronunciation and word details. 浏览释义、例句，可听发音并查看详情。
- **Practice / 练习:** Answer questions with immediate feedback and revisit mistakes. 答题后即时反馈，并复习错词。
- **Cards / 卡片:** Flip, then use Know/Again or swipe. Reverse the card direction in Settings. 翻卡后选择记住/再练或滑动；设置可更换卡片正反方向。
- **Test / 测试:** Choose 10, 20 or all words when available. Move between questions, then finish for results and a wrong-word retest. No answer feedback before completion. 可选择 10、20 或全部词（视单元词数）；前后查看题目，完成后查看成绩并重测错词。提交前不显示正误。
- **HSE Advanced Practice / 高中进阶练习:** Available within supported HSE packages; follow the task-specific prompts. 在支持的高中词汇包内进入，并按题目说明作答。

## Reading and school sections / 阅读与学校题型

Choose a Reading Explorer book, or Middle School → grade → MCQ/Reading A–E/Cloze. Choose **Study** to browse exercises freely. Choose **Test** for a random exercise: unseen draws first, then the least-used; it will not immediately repeat the last/current test if another is available. Submit for results, then choose Next exercise/article. Study and Test answers are kept separately for Reading Explorer.

选择 Reading Explorer 书本，或初中英语 → 年级 → 单选/阅读 A–E/完形。**学习**可自由选题；**测试**随机抽题，未抽过优先，再抽次数较少的题；有其他题可选时不连续重复当前/上次题目。提交后可做下一篇。Reading Explorer 的学习与测试答案分别保存。

Reading Explorer questions without verified answer keys save your answers for teacher review, not an invented score. Reading Response and Writing are not open in the learner app. High-school reading remains unavailable; existing HSE vocabulary is under Vocabulary.

Reading Explorer 理解题暂无核实的答案，保存后请老师批阅，不会生成虚构分数。阅读表达与写作暂不开放。高中阅读题库尚未上线；现有高中词汇包在词汇入口中。

## In-passage meanings / 原文释义

In Study, double-tap a dotted word/phrase to save a highlight and fold its meaning open in the passage. Tap an already highlighted word once to close/reopen. Undo removes the latest highlight but retains encounter history. Only terms with authored offline definitions are selectable; basic function words are excluded. Settings offers nine highlight colours. Test shows no word lookup, definition controls or saved highlights.

学习中双击点状下划线词/短语，即可保存标记并在原文中折叠展开释义。单击已标记词可收起/重开。撤销只移除最近标记，保留接触记录。仅有离线释义的词可选，常见功能词不包含在内。设置可选九种颜色。测试不显示查词、释义按钮或已存标记。

For Cloze, tap the numbered blank to unfold options. In Study, **tap the letter to answer**; double-tap dotted option words to look them up without selecting an answer. In Test, choose the option normally; feedback appears only after submission.

完形中点击编号空格展开选项。学习时**点击字母作答**；双击选项中的点状下划线词可查词，不会选中答案。测试时正常选择选项，提交后才显示反馈。

## Memory Palace / 记忆宫殿

Saved vocabulary with meanings appears here. Review due items, reveal answers, and record whether you remembered them. Encounters and review history are local to this device. A larger redesign remains planned, not delivered.

有释义的已存生词出现在此。复习到期词、揭示答案并记录是否记住。接触和复习记录保存在本机。更大范围的重设计仍待规划，并未上线。

## Settings, updates and offline use / 设置、更新与离线

Home Settings is at the top left. Change English/中文, name, display inversion, card direction and highlight colour; manage audio, check updates, replay the tutorial, export/restore progress and view **Updates & fixes**. The log has its own English/中文 switch. The 1.10.1 update notice appears once when that version first opens; dismissing it is remembered on this device. The log remains available in Settings.

设置在首页左上角。可更改语言、姓名、反色、卡片方向和标记色，管理音频、检查更新、重看指南、导出/恢复进度，以及查看**更新与修复记录**。记录可独立切换中英文。首次打开 1.10.1 时显示一次更新提示，关闭后本机记住；设置中仍可查看记录。

In the Apple PWA, Download all vocabulary audio uses one verified pack. Reading audio is optional—download it while online before offline use. Android packaging is designed to include word audio and omit reading audio; the 1.10.1 Android build is currently on hold. The narrator replacement was cancelled, so current recordings remain. Export a backup before reinstalling or changing devices: there is no cloud account or immutable server-side history.

Apple PWA 的下载全部词汇音频使用一个校验音频包。阅读音频可选下载，离线前请联网下载。安卓打包方案内置单词音频、不内置阅读音频；1.10.1 安卓构建现已暂停。已取消更换朗读者，保留现有录音。重装或换设备前请导出备份：应用没有云端账号或不可修改的服务器记录。
